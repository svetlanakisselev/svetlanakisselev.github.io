Wearable Baroque Reliquary Art by Svetlana Kisselev

Guarding a kingdom of wonders — a place where imagination is free to become real.

Svetlana Kisselev creates one-of-a-kind wearable sculptures — reliquary necklaces and pectoral pieces combining Orthodox iconography, Baroque opulence and African ceremonial art. Each piece is hand-made in mixed media, numbered, and issued with a certificate of authenticity. Based in Paris, her work is created for collectors, curators and private commissions worldwide.

Every piece is a story to be told — a personal way to love life. Worn, rather than only imagined.
