Inspired by Orientalist Dreams, Statement Necklace “1001 Nights — Gilded Captivity”

Legendary Oriental opulence that binds as much as it adorns.

Stylized Ceremonial Necklace · One of a Kind · Paris 2026
A narrative piece that explores splendor as bondage, and freedom as a choice.

Measurements:
Pendant (excluding clasp): W=6 cm, H=28 cm
Chain: L=41 cm

Techniques used: Mixed media

Description of materials:
Brass stampings, gold-tone filigree components, 24k matt gold plated findings (beads, spacer beads, chain). Other mixed metal findings. Faceted glass stones and cabochons in turquoise, purple, and teal, pearl accents, dangling gold stars. The paper imagery is hand-treated to replicate the look of aged, crackled oil paintings.
